Overview

Climate disinformation spreads faster than fact-checking can answer. Much of it starts in local circuits: public works, floods, energy tariffs, wildfires. A central team does not reach those circuits, and those who do reach them have neither the method nor the time to verify.

The Alliance calls on organizations that already produce or distribute information about climate to check together. The Alliance brings the verification method, the training and the network of checkers. The partner organization brings knowledge of the territory, distribution channels and part of the cost.

Each partnership is designed per project. Co-funding, volume of checks and publication channels are agreed between the parties before signing. Every published check carries the names of both organizations.

What is supported

  • Communication organizations, media collectives, local outlets and research centers working on climate, energy or the environment.
  • Fact-checking projects about a defined territory or topic, lasting at least six months.
  • Proposals that publish the checks on the organization's own channels, in addition to the Alliance repository.
  • Organizations incorporated in Brazil, with an active CNPJ.

What is not supported

  • Checking of electoral content unrelated to climate.
  • Projects that will not publish the checks under the network's method and labels.
  • Organizations that produce paid content for energy or mining companies.

Selection criteria

The criteria are listed in order of weight.

1.

Presence in the territory or topic

Track record of coverage and active channels with the public that the disinformation reaches.

2.

Fact-checking capacity

Team available to verify, with or without prior experience, and willingness to adopt the network's method.

3.

Reach of publication

Where the checks will be published and how many people they are likely to reach.

4.

Sustainability of the partnership

Financial or staff contribution the organization can sustain over the life of the project.
