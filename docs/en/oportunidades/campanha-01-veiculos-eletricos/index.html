Overview

The campaign exists to accelerate the electrification of the Brazilian fleet. The obstacle is not only price: it is what people believe about price. Charging costs, real range, maintenance and resale value are settled in everyday conversation, and that is where the campaign has to be.

Instead of hiring a single influencer to repeat a ready message, the call buys design capacity from several creators at once. Each selected project pursues the same objective through its own strategy, format and narrative, and directs its audience to the campaign participation channel.

INDICA runs the call as responsible intermediary and shares delivery with Koko.

What is supported

  • Original projects that generate public conversation about the cost of electric vehicles in Brazil.
  • Projects that direct the audience to the campaign participation channel.
  • Creators as individuals or companies, small collectives and communication agencies.
  • Any audience size and any niche: eligibility belongs to the project, not to the channel's usual subject.
  • Written or video proposals, with an itemized budget.

Selection criteria

The criteria are listed in order of weight.

1.

Project quality

Strength of the idea, the narrative and the format proposed.

2.

Expected reach relative to the creator's scale

What is assessed is the reach expected given the audience size of whoever proposes, not the size itself.

3.

Strength of the conversion path

How well the project takes the audience to the campaign participation channel.

4.

Feasibility

Coherence between scope, budget and schedule.

Panel

The panel brings together members of INDICA, a representative of the funder and invited creators. Its composition is published before applications close.
