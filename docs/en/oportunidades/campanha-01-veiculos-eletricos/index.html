Overview

Instead of hiring a single influencer to front the campaign, this call buys creative capacity from several creators at once. Each selected project serves the campaign objective through the strategy, the format and the narrative of whoever proposed it.

Three to ten independent projects reach different audiences through different doors, and the campaign result does not depend on one person. Selection is a public process, with criteria and result published.

INDICA runs the call as the responsible intermediary and shares execution with Koko.

What is supported

  • Original projects that generate public conversation about the cost of electric vehicles in Brazil.
  • Projects that direct the audience to the campaign participation channel.
  • Creators as individuals or companies, small collectives and communication agencies.
  • Any audience size and any niche: eligibility belongs to the project, not to the channel's usual subject.
  • Written or video proposals, with an itemized budget.

Frequently asked questions

Question 01

Who can apply?

Creators as individuals or companies, small collectives and communication agencies, of any audience size and any niche. Eligibility belongs to the project.

Question 02

Does the program approve scripts or cuts?

No. Approval happens at selection: what is delivered is the approved proposal. INDICA's coordination covers schedule, technical information and support.

Question 03

Do I need to disclose advertising?

Yes, from the first post on, under CONAR rules. Narrative devices of buildup and reveal are welcome, as long as the paid nature of the content is never hidden.

Question 04

Who holds the intellectual property?

The creator, with a non-exclusive institutional license. The content stays published for at least 12 months.

Question 05

I have had a contract with a carmaker or a fuel company. Does that rule me out?

Not automatically. Shortlisted applicants go through a conflict of interest check, and declaring commercial relationships from the last 24 months with the automotive, fuel and energy sectors is mandatory. Omitting a relationship rules you out.

Question 06

How can the proposal be sent?

In writing or on video, with the creator's introduction and audience metrics, the project, the conversion path, the schedule and the itemized budget.
