Overview

The fellowship funds six creators for six months to produce a series from public databases. LabDados provides training, guidance and access to the databases; the creator brings the audience and the language they already command. What is expected at the end is a series published on the fellow's channel, not a report.

Each fellow receives R$ 15,000 in six monthly installments of R$ 2,500 and follows a work plan settled in the first month: one topic, one main database and a publication calendar. A LabDados advisor accompanies the fellow in fortnightly meetings and reviews the reading of the data before each publication.

The fellowship includes enrollment in the course Science communication for creators, from the program's library, taken in the first two months. Completing the course is a condition for receiving the installments from the third onward.

What is supported

  • Creators with a channel active for at least one year, on any platform and with any audience size.
  • A proposal for a series on a topic of public interest that depends on reading data: budget, health, education, public safety, climate, labor or transport.
  • Use of Brazilian public databases, with the database and the intended cut identified in the proposal.
  • Any format: video, audio, text, newsletter or carousel.
  • Part-time dedication, compatible with the channel's routine, with at least one piece published per month from the third month onward.

What is not supported

  • A proposal from an organization, news outlet or agency. The fellowship is individual.
  • A series based on private company data or on the creator's own survey without a verifiable public database.
  • A creator with an employment relationship or a current contract with LabDados.

Selection criteria

The criteria are listed in order of weight.

1.

Relevance of the topic

Public interest of the subject and the existence of a database able to sustain it.

2.

Ability to translate

The channel's track record in explaining complex subjects to a non-specialist audience, shown by pieces already published.

3.

Feasibility of the plan

Coherence between topic, database, publication calendar and the routine of the person proposing.

4.

Composition of the cohort

Distribution of regions, platforms and topics among the six selected. Applied as a tiebreaker after the three previous criteria.
