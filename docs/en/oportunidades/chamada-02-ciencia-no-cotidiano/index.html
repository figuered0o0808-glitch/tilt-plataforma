Overview

Science reaches people through small decisions: what to eat, how to treat a fever, whether a new light bulb is worth it, whom to believe when two videos say the opposite. The people who explain this well are concentrated in a few channels and a few formats, and most of the audience never gets to them.

The call funds creators who bring that explanation to their own audience, in the format and on the platform where they already publish. Fundação Vale do Rio Claro funds the call with R$ 240,000. INDICA proposes and runs the call, conducts the selection with the panel and follows production through to publication.

The theme is broad on purpose: any field of science qualifies, as long as the project starts from something the audience decides or does. A creator who already publishes about cooking, personal finance, parenting or games is as eligible as one who publishes about science.

What is supported

  • Original projects that explain the science behind everyday decisions: food, health, home, money, climate, technology.
  • Any format and any platform: short video, podcast, newsletter, post series, live stream.
  • Creators as individuals or companies, of any audience size and any niche.
  • Series of at least four pieces, published between January and March 2027.
  • Written or video proposals, with an itemized budget and at least one scientific source per planned piece.

What is not supported

  • Content that promotes a specific product, supplement, treatment or brand.
  • Dissemination of research results not yet published or not peer-reviewed.
  • Event coverage and institutional material for universities, companies or governments.

Selection criteria

The criteria are listed in order of weight.

1.

Accuracy of the scientific content

Verifiable sources and a correct reading of what the research says and what it cannot yet say.

2.

Link to an everyday decision

The project starts from something the audience decides or does, not from the topic for its own sake.

3.

Fit between format and audience

Coherence between the format, the platform and the public the creator already reaches.

4.

Feasibility

Coherence between scope, budget and schedule.
