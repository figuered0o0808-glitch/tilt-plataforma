Overview

Latin American cities live with both too little and too much water. Rationing and flooding coexist in the same neighborhood, sometimes in the same year. The research on this exists; what is missing is content that translates it for people deciding where to live, what to demand and how to prepare.

The call is open to creators and organizations from any Latin American country. The theme is broad on purpose: each proposal chooses its angle, format and audience. What is required is that the project start from a concrete city and from data that can be verified.

Observatório Clima Urbano coordinates the selection and opens its database and contact with its researchers to the selected projects. Fundação Vale do Rio Claro funds the grants. The ten projects will be presented at a virtual meeting at the end of the cycle.

What is supported

  • Creators, collectives and organizations based in Latin American countries.
  • Projects on water supply, sanitation, floods, urban rivers or water consumption in a defined city.
  • Any format: video, audio, text, data visualization, social media series.
  • Proposals in Portuguese or Spanish, with a budget and a publication schedule.

What is not supported

  • Projects on rural or agricultural water with no link to a city.
  • Content commissioned by sanitation utilities or companies in the sector.
  • Academic research with no publication plan for the general public.

Selection criteria

The criteria are listed in order of weight.

1.

Local relevance

How much the topic weighs in the chosen city and how well the proposal demonstrates it.

2.

Grounding in data

Use of verifiable data, with a declared source for each central claim of the project.

3.

Fit between format and audience

Coherence between the chosen format and the people the project wants to reach.

4.

Feasibility

Coherence between scope, budget in local currency and publication deadline.
