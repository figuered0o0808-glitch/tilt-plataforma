An economic model for the content market on algorithmic social networks

Francisco Figueiredo, Lauanny Brandão, Pedro Bordinhão

A theory of timing in the dispute for attention: latent demand, active supply and the optimal publication window. It treats attention as a scarce resource and derives the condition that closes the window, when active supply starts growing faster than demand.

Price formation in the influence market

Francisco Figueiredo

Why the price of sponsored content lies above production costs and varies across similar creators. The paper examines reputational stock and bargaining between the creator’s costs and risks and what the advertiser can and wants to pay.

The economic design of the influence market

Francisco Figueiredo

Introduction to the series. It describes the participants in the influence market, what each one gives and receives, and where attention and money flow. It presents the fields covered by the series.

Whoever pays the piper calls the tune

Francisco Figueiredo, Cecília Moreyra de Figueiredo

An analysis by INDICA in partnership with Entremídias, the Media Literacy Lab of the Journalism program at UFRRJ, on betting advertising, influencers’ discourse and regulation of the influencer market.

Report: how to measure public interest campaigns

Beatriz Nakamura, Rafael Ordoñez, Lívia Carvalho Mota

Which indicators show whether a campaign changed what people know or do, and why reach and likes do not answer that question.

Technical note: fact-checking before publishing

Renata Quiroga, Tomás Albuquerque

A verification routine for numbers, quotes and images, sized for the time a creator has between recording and publishing.
