An economic model for the content market in algorithmic social networks

Why two similar pieces of content yield very different results depending on when they go live, and what that changes in campaign planning.

Who it is for

For organizations that want to position themselves better on social media, take opportunities when they appear and plan campaigns with influencers.

What you take from it

Deciding fast is worth more than producing fast. The time between spotting the opportunity and publishing is made of decision and production, and decision is the part you can shorten.

When the campaign pays per contracted piece, the best moment to publish is before the peak of public attention, not during it.

Competition for attention does not come only from what was published today: old content keeps circulating and taking up space.
