Campaigns with organizations: from briefing to measurement

Three weeks on the full cycle of a campaign between a creator and an organization: what the briefing must contain, what the contract defines, how content gets approved and how results are measured.

Class open

Campaigns between creators and organizations tend to fail at predictable points: a vague briefing, an undefined approval deadline, a metric agreed only after publication. The course walks through each stage using real documents from campaigns run by INDICA and Estúdio Paralelo, with the names changed.

Cohorts mix creators and organization staff. In the exercises, each side writes down what it would expect from the other, and the comparison is discussed live. The aim is for both sides to leave knowing what to ask for and what to offer.

Offered by INDICA with Estúdio Paralelo. Two live classes a week, recorded, and one final deliverable: a complete briefing and a measurement plan for a real or simulated campaign.

Who it is for

  • Creators who have done paid partnerships and want to work with nonprofits, institutes and foundations.
  • Communication and project managers at organizations that hire or plan to hire creators.
  • Small production companies and agencies that broker campaigns and need to standardize briefings and reports.

Course program

4 modules
  1. 01

    Briefing

    What the organization needs to tell the creator before any proposal: objective, audience, constraints and what cannot be said.

  2. 02

    Proposal and contract

    Scope, deliverables, approval deadlines, usage rights and what happens when one side is late.

  3. 03

    Production and approval

    Script, final video and revisions: how many rounds, who decides and how each approval is recorded.

  4. 04

    Measurement and reporting

    Set the metric before publishing, collect data over the two weeks that follow and write a report the organization can use.

Duration
3 weeks
